          How it works

          1. FindThe agent works out what you are — brand, venue, creator or podcast — and resolves you to a Qloo entity.
          2. ListenIt asks Qloo what your audience also loves: podcasts, brands, artists, films, and nearby places for venues.
          3. ScreenExplicit, partisan or extremist results are held back from ad and partner picks — and counted, never hidden. The screen is automatic and can miss things.
          4. ActA writer model turns the data into a plan. It may only cite Qloo results by id; anything it invents is removed in code.

          The AI guess tab is the control: the same model, asked the same questions without data. Where the two lists differ, the plan is coming from your audience, not from stereotypes.

          Use it from your own agent

          The same tools are an MCP server (Streamable HTTP): /mcp — find_entity, audience_affinities, audience_action_plan.

          AI disclosure. Plans and email drafts are written by Google Gemini (gemini-3.1-pro-preview; research and screening by gemini-3.5-flash). Audience data comes from Qloo's taste graph and shows affinities — what people who like you also like — not verified facts about your customers.

          Ideas and drafts for reflection and planning only — not marketing, legal or financial advice. Nothing is sent from here; you edit and send drafts yourself.

          Privacy. What you type is sent to Google Gemini and Qloo to produce your run; we do not keep it. No accounts, cookies or analytics. Your IP address and request times are held in memory for up to 24 hours for rate limiting only.
